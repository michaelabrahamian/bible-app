# Reader guide

The **Bible** tab lists books in your primary translation. Select a book to open its first available chapter. The reader's arrows move through available chapters; the header button switches between one translation and side-by-side comparison.

The **Settings** tab chooses the primary translation, comparison translation, and dual-view preference. Translation choices, comparison preference, and reading position are saved on the device.

The current app bundles KJV and a partial Eastern Armenian text (Genesis, Exodus, and the four Gospels). Entirely empty Armenian books and chapters are hidden; 17 partly populated chapters with missing text and unreliable references are withheld. Missing comparison content is shown with an explanation, while KJV remains in its own pane. Both panes reset to the top when the passage changes. It has no search, bookmarks, audio, account sync, daily verse, or home-screen widgets yet. See [README.md](README.md) for setup and [data/README.md](data/README.md) for content limitations and provenance.
