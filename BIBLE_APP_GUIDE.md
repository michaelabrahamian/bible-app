# Reader guide

The **Bible** tab lists books in your primary translation. Select a book to open its first available chapter. The reader's arrows move through available chapters; the header button switches between one translation and side-by-side comparison.

The **Settings** tab chooses the primary translation, comparison translation, and dual-view preference. Translation choices, comparison preference, and reading position are saved on the device.

The current app bundles KJV and a partial Eastern Armenian text (Genesis, Exodus, and the four Gospels). Unavailable Armenian books and chapters are hidden. It has no search, bookmarks, audio, account sync, daily verse, or home-screen widgets yet. See [README.md](README.md) for setup and [data/README.md](data/README.md) for content limitations and provenance.
